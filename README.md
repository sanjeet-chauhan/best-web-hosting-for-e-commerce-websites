## How to Migrate an Existing Store

Moving an active store requires more care than moving a normal website because orders and customer data may continue changing during the migration.

A safer process is:

1. Take a fresh backup.
2. Set up the new hosting account.
3. Transfer website files.
4. Transfer the database.
5. Transfer required email accounts.
6. Check WordPress and WooCommerce configuration.
7. Test the new copy before changing DNS.
8. Confirm products, customers, orders, and checkout.
9. Schedule the final synchronization.
10. Update DNS.
11. Monitor the store after the DNS change.

YouStable offers free migration on eligible WordPress plans. Check the current migration terms before requesting the transfer.

## Backups Are Critical for E-commerce

Your product images can be replaced. Your orders and customer information are much harder to recreate.

YouStable's WordPress hosting currently includes weekly backups and one click restore. However, an active store should not depend on a single backup location.

A sensible backup strategy should include:

- Website files
- WooCommerce database
- Product images
- Customer related data
- Configuration files
- Important emails
- A copy stored outside the hosting account

Before major WooCommerce, theme, plugin, or WordPress updates, take an additional backup.

## Common Hosting Mistakes E-commerce Owners Make

### Choosing the Cheapest Plan Automatically

A cheap plan can be perfectly fine for a small store, but 1GB RAM may become restrictive as plugins, products, visitors, and database activity increase.

### Installing Too Many Plugins

Every plugin adds functionality, but poorly optimized or unnecessary plugins can increase database queries, PHP processing, and page load time.

### Ignoring Database Growth

WooCommerce stores continuously generate data.

Orders, customer records, sessions, logs, product information, and plugin data can make the database much more important than the number of products alone.

### Using Hosting Email for Everything

Shared hosting email limits may not be suitable for high volume transactional communication.

For important order messages, use a reliable SMTP or transactional email provider.

### Waiting Until the Store Crashes

Do not wait for customers to complain before checking resource usage.

Watch your CPU, RAM, storage, database size, traffic patterns, and response times so you can upgrade before a major sales event.

### Forgetting Mobile Checkout

A store can look perfect on desktop and still have a broken mobile checkout.

Always test the complete purchase journey on a phone.
